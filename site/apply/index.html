Solutions

One process, four sectors.

The steps stay the same; the rules change. Here is what Art Prove does for each kind of client.

Hospitality

Hospitality

Hotels, resorts, restaurants and bars

The challenge
Art is often the last line of the fit-out budget and ends up generic, while guests expect a place they will remember and photograph.
What Art Prove does
Matches the brand and each zone — lobby, rooms, F&B, spa — with artists whose practice fits; plans series for rooms and exhibitions in residence.
The result
A signature piece and a room programme that read as the brand, sourced in weeks, with sales of works to guests as an extra revenue line.
Deliverables
  • Lobby signature piece concept
  • Room and corridor series plan
  • Exhibition-in-residence programme
  • QR labels and sales flow
Real Estate

Real Estate

Developers, residential and mixed-use

The challenge
Public art is added late, has to pass engineering, safety and city approvals, and must still say something about the project.
What Art Prove does
Links the project's values to a concrete object, checks it against the site — base, weight, fire access, weather — and plans approvals and installation.
The result
A public-art piece that supports the sales story and survives the courtyard, delivered on the construction schedule.
Deliverables
  • Art concept tied to project values
  • Object spec with site constraints
  • Staged plan and budget
  • Artist and fabricator contracts
Brands

Brands

Retail, consumer and corporate brands

The challenge
Collaborations are slow to set up and risky: the wrong artist, unclear rights, or a piece that does not read as the brand.
What Art Prove does
Finds artists by brand DNA, drafts collaboration formats — installations, limited editions, campaign pieces — and lists the rights to clear.
The result
A collaboration with a clear fit, clear rights and a plan for production and launch.
Deliverables
  • Shortlist with brand-fit reasons
  • Collaboration concepts
  • Rights and licensing checklist
  • Production and launch plan
Architecture & Design

Architecture & Design

Interior and architecture studios

The challenge
Studios need art that fits the drawings — sizes, materials, budgets — without weeks of gallery emails.
What Art Prove does
Gives the studio a curated bench and specs it can drop into the project, with artist approval before the client sees prices.
The result
Faster art packages for client presentations, with real works, sizes and checked materials.
Deliverables
  • Curated shortlist per project
  • Specs: size, material, weight
  • Client-ready proposal
  • Commission management
