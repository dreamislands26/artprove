What we measure in every pilot

Time from brief to an approved concept (target 14 days), rounds of revisions (2 or fewer), cost vs. a consultant (40% lower or more), and guest mentions of art and design in reviews and on Instagram, 3 months before and after install. No published study yet proves that art raises hotel room rates by a set percent, so we track it ourselves.
