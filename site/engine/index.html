Changed after review

  • Maria's “portrait”. The photo was the p. 11 catalogue image of an unidentified person with Blondie. Replaced with a work photo; the artist record now says no verified portrait.
  • Track record inside an AI draft. Moved to Artist facts with pages (The Standard Bangkok, PDF p. 91; AVANI+ Riverside, PDF p. 81). The validator now rejects names that are not in the spec's facts and rejects track record inside AI text.
  • Briefs not marked as demo. create_brief takes hypothetical=true; every card is labelled as a hypothetical brief and its values are tagged Assumption.
  • Bridge without evidence. Each axis now needs its own quote, cut at sentence boundaries: sub-brand page first, then the group purpose, then the brief's goals. No evidence, no bridge.
  • Sizes copied from old works. With a space size, new works are sized from the wall or floor. Without one, the size is labelled “by analogy with <work>, page” and Interpretation.
  • Material checks. Added acrylic/polymer, PVC foam board and canvas-paint rules; all pre-production items are now tagged Needs consent.
  • Page numbers. Standard: PDF page (opens directly), with the printed page shown next to it where found. Artist catalogue pages are PDF pages.
  • Fact without a page. Artist themes now carry their page (Maria p. 2, Supmanee p. 3, Chayanich p. 3).
